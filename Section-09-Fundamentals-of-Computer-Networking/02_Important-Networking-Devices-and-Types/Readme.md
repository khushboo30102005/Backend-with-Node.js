# ✅ Important Networking Devices


***Common Networking Devices are:-***

---

## ✨host

Any device that can **consume (receive) data and also produce (send) data**.

---

## ✨repeater

It is used to **regenerate and amplify weak signals** when the distance is too long, so that data can travel further.

---

## ✨hub

Used for connecting multiple computers using one device.  
The request goes to **all computers in the network**, and is ignored by the unTargeted computers.

---

## ✨switch

Used for connecting multiple computers with one device, but the request goes **only to the targeted computer** using MAC address.

---

## ✨router

It is used to **connect multiple networks (like LAN to Internet)**.  
It can be wired or wireless and works using IP address.  
It is a powerful device and contains a **small computer inside it (CPU, memory, OS)**.

---

## ✨bridge

Used to **connect two LAN networks and filter traffic** using MAC address.  
It was used in older days; now mostly replaced by switches and routers.

---

## ✨modem

A device that **modulates and demodulates signals**.  
It converts signals between **analog and digital form** for internet communication.

---

## ✨access-point

It is used to **extend the range of a wireless network (Wi-Fi coverage)**.

---

## ✨node

Any device connected to a network is called a node.  
It can **send, receive, or forward data**.

# ✅ Types of Network


***These are important types of networks:-***

---

## ✨LAN (Local Area Network)

The network is limited to a **small area** like buildings, homes, or offices.

---

## ✨MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

This network covers a **city or metropolitan area**.

---

## ✨WAN (Wide Area Network)

This network covers a **large geographical area**, such as countries or even the whole world (e.g., Internet).

---

## ✨CAN (Campus Area Network)

The network is spread across a **campus** like universities, colleges, or large organizations.

---

## ✨PAN (Personal Area Network)

The network is limited to a **very small area (within a room)**.  
It connects personal devices like **PC, laptop, smartphone, smartwatch, smart TV**.

---

## ✨BAN (Body Area Network)

The network is limited **around a person’s body**, such as **smartwatches, headphones, fitness bands**.

---

## ✨SAN (Storage Area Network)

It connects **multiple storage devices** to provide high-speed data access and centralized storage.
